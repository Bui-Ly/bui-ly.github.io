Website data

BuiLy Apps does not use its own analytics, advertising trackers, user accounts, contact forms, or cookies on this website. You can browse the public pages without providing personal information directly to BuiLy Apps.

Support messages

If you email support, BuiLy Apps receives the email address and any information you choose to include, such as your name, device details, screenshots, app version, or issue description. This information is used to investigate the request, reply to you, prevent abuse, and maintain a reasonable support history.

Please do not send passwords, full payment card numbers, banking information, or unrelated sensitive information.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may process technical information necessary to deliver and secure the website, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own terms and privacy statement. BuiLy Apps does not receive a visitor analytics dashboard from this site.

Retention and security

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, follow up, maintain support records, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Reasonable measures are used to protect received information, but no email or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information you previously sent through the support channel, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. For an in-app account, use the account deletion instructions for that specific app.
