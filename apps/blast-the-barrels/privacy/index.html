Privacy policy

Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run

This policy explains how information is processed when you play Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run, including data used to provide and measure advertising through Unity LevelPlay mediation and information used for Google Play in-app purchases.

Scope

This policy applies to Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run, an Android game published by Nguyen Bui Ly under BuiLy Apps. It does not replace the privacy terms of Google Play, Unity, or advertising providers that independently process information.

Privacy summary: Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run does not require an account or ask for your name, phone number, or email address during normal gameplay. The game contains ads and offers optional in-app purchases. Advertising providers may process device, approximate location, diagnostic, and ad-interaction data. Google Play and Unity Purchasing process purchase information when you buy an item.

Information processed

Gameplay data stored on your device

The game saves settings, progress, in-game currency, upgrades, and purchase entitlements locally so that gameplay can continue between sessions. It also stores a purchase transaction identifier locally to prevent the same purchase from being credited twice. This local data remains on your device unless you choose to include it in a support request.

Advertising and device data

Unity LevelPlay and participating advertising partners may collect or receive approximate location derived from network information, device or advertising identifiers, app and device information, interactions with ads, and diagnostic information. The exact data available can depend on your device, region, privacy choices, and the advertising network that serves an ad.

In-app purchases

If you choose to buy in-game currency or remove ads, Google Play processes the purchase and provides a receipt to Unity Purchasing in the game. The game verifies the receipt and records the transaction identifier and resulting entitlement on your device. BuiLy Apps does not receive your full payment card number through the game. Google Play and Unity may process purchase-related information under their own policies.

Support communications

If you contact BuiLy Apps, we receive your email address and any text, screenshots, recordings, device details, or other information you choose to provide.

How information is used

  • Run the game and preserve local settings or progress.
  • Select, deliver, limit, and measure advertisements.
  • Verify purchases, grant purchased items, restore eligible entitlements, and prevent duplicate grants.
  • Measure ad performance and understand interactions with ads.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, invalid traffic, and security problems.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve stability.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

Advertising and third-party services

Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run uses Unity LevelPlay as an advertising mediation platform. Mediation allows an ad to be supplied by Unity or a participating third-party advertising network. The network that serves an ad may process information under its own privacy policy.

For current details, review Unity’s LevelPlay Google Data Safety guidance and Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob is enabled as a mediated ad network in the current Android project. For its data practices, see the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases use Google Play Billing through Unity Purchasing. See the Google Play Terms and Unity privacy policy for their processing of purchase information.

The set of participating networks may change as advertising configuration is updated. Only networks configured for the version of the game you use are eligible to serve ads.

How information is shared

Advertising data may be transmitted to Unity LevelPlay and the advertising network selected to serve or measure an ad. Purchase receipts are processed by Google Play and Unity Purchasing to complete and verify optional in-app purchases. These providers may use the data for advertising, analytics, frequency capping, attribution, security, and fraud prevention in accordance with their policies and applicable law.

BuiLy Apps does not sell the information you send directly in a support email. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, or investigate misuse.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local game data, including purchase transaction identifiers and entitlements, remains on your device until it is cleared through device settings or the game is uninstalled. Google Play may retain purchase history independently, and eligible purchases may be restored through the store. Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain security records, or meet legal obligations.

Advertising providers determine their own retention periods and deletion procedures. You may use Unity’s privacy resources or contact BuiLy Apps for help locating the appropriate request process.

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, including encrypted transmission where supported by the provider. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s privacy

Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13. BuiLy Apps does not knowingly request personal information from children through the game. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information can contact us to request review and deletion.

Your privacy choices

  • Use Android and Google account privacy settings to control or reset the advertising identifier and available ad-personalization choices.
  • Use any consent or privacy controls presented in the game where required or available in your region.
  • Clear the game’s local storage or uninstall the game to remove locally stored settings and progress.
  • Contact BuiLy Apps to request access to or deletion of information you submitted directly through support.

Limiting advertising data may result in less personalized advertising but does not necessarily remove ads.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests, email nguyenbuily.it@gmail.com. Include “Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run” in the subject, but do not include passwords, payment card details, or government identification.

This policy may be updated when the game, advertising partners, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
