Privacy policy

Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run

This single privacy policy covers the Android and iOS versions of Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run, including advertising through Unity LevelPlay mediation and optional purchases through Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Scope

This policy applies to Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run, a game for Android and iOS (including iPadOS) published by Nguyen Bui Ly under BuiLy Apps. Both versions use this policy and this URL; the store and privacy controls that apply to you depend on your device. It does not replace the privacy terms of Google Play, Apple, Unity, or advertising providers that independently process information.

Privacy summary: Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run does not require an account or ask for your name, phone number, or email address during normal gameplay. The game contains ads and offers optional in-app purchases. Advertising providers may process device, approximate location, diagnostic, and ad-interaction data. Google Play (Android), the Apple App Store (iOS), and Unity Purchasing process purchase information when you buy an item.

Information processed

Gameplay data stored on your device

The game saves settings, progress, in-game currency, upgrades, and purchase entitlements locally so that gameplay can continue between sessions. It also stores a purchase transaction identifier locally to prevent the same purchase from being credited twice. We do not operate a game account or a game-progress cloud-sync service. If enabled, your device or store backup settings may include local game data in backups managed by Google or Apple. You may also choose to include information in a support request.

Advertising and device data

Unity LevelPlay and participating advertising partners may collect or receive approximate location derived from network information, device or advertising identifiers, app and device information, interactions with ads, and diagnostic information. The exact data available can depend on your device, region, privacy choices, and the advertising network that serves an ad.

In-app purchases

If you choose to buy in-game currency or remove ads, Google Play Billing on Android or Apple StoreKit on iOS processes the purchase through Unity Purchasing. The relevant store verifies the purchase and provides transaction information so the game can grant the item and check eligible entitlements. The game records the transaction identifier and resulting entitlement on your device to prevent duplicate grants. Eligible non-consumable purchases, such as Remove Ads, can be restored; consumed currency purchases are not granted again through Restore Purchases. BuiLy Apps does not receive your full payment card number through the game. Google, Apple, and Unity may process purchase-related information under their own policies.

Support communications

If you contact BuiLy Apps, we receive your email address and any text, screenshots, recordings, device details, or other information you choose to provide.

How information is used

  • Run the game and preserve local settings or progress.
  • Select, deliver, limit, and measure advertisements.
  • Verify purchases, grant purchased items, restore eligible entitlements, and prevent duplicate grants.
  • Measure ad performance and understand interactions with ads.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, invalid traffic, and security problems.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve stability.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

Advertising and third-party services

Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run uses Unity LevelPlay as an advertising mediation platform. Mediation allows an ad to be supplied by Unity or a participating third-party advertising network. The network that serves an ad may process information under its own privacy policy.

For current details, review Unity’s LevelPlay Google Data Safety guidance and Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

The game integrates Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and ironSource through Unity LevelPlay mediation. The iOS version also includes ironSource Ad Quality for ad quality measurement and diagnostics. For its data practices, see the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases use Google Play Billing on Android and Apple StoreKit on iOS through Unity Purchasing. See the Google Play Terms, Apple Privacy Policy, and Unity privacy policy for their processing of purchase information.

The set of participating networks may change as advertising configuration is updated. Only networks configured for the version of the game you use are eligible to serve ads.

How information is shared

Advertising data may be transmitted to Unity LevelPlay and the advertising network selected to serve or measure an ad. Purchase and transaction information is processed by Google Play or Apple and Unity Purchasing to complete and verify optional in-app purchases. These providers may use the data for advertising, analytics, frequency capping, attribution, security, and fraud prevention in accordance with their policies and applicable law.

BuiLy Apps does not sell the information you send directly in a support email. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, or investigate misuse.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local game data, including purchase transaction identifiers and entitlements, remains on your device until it is deleted through device settings or the app is deleted. Device backups may retain or restore this data according to your Google or Apple backup settings. Google Play and Apple may retain purchase history independently, and eligible purchases may be restored through the store. Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain security records, or meet legal obligations.

Advertising providers determine their own retention periods and deletion procedures. You may use Unity’s privacy resources or contact BuiLy Apps for help locating the appropriate request process.

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, including encrypted transmission where supported by the provider. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s privacy

Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13. BuiLy Apps does not knowingly request personal information from children through the game. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information can contact us to request review and deletion.

Your privacy choices

  • On Android, use device and Google account privacy settings to control or reset the advertising identifier and available ad-personalization choices.
  • On iOS, we request permission through App Tracking Transparency (ATT) before tracking your activity across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized advertising or ad measurement. Advertising identifiers are used for this tracking only when permitted. You can change permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Declining tracking does not prevent normal gameplay or require a purchase.
  • On iOS, Google’s User Messaging Platform checks whether a regional consent message is needed before the advertising SDK initializes. Where required, open Settings in the game and select Privacy Choices to review or change your choices. Consent choices and regulatory signals are processed by Google and participating advertising partners.
  • Use any consent or privacy controls presented in the game where required or available in your region.
  • On Android, clear the game’s app storage or uninstall it to remove local settings and progress. On iOS, use Delete App to remove the app and its local data; Offload App retains its documents and data. Manage any retained device backups in your Google or Apple account settings.
  • Contact BuiLy Apps to request access to or deletion of information you submitted directly through support.

Limiting advertising data may result in less personalized advertising but does not necessarily remove ads.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests, email nguyenbuily.it@gmail.com. Include “Blast the Barrels: Infinite Run” in the subject, but do not include passwords, payment card details, or government identification.

This policy may be updated when the game, advertising partners, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
