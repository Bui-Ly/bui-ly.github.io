Pack Sort: Box Puzzle

This policy explains how information is processed when you play Pack Sort: Box Puzzle, including game data saved on your device and information processed to provide advertising through Unity LevelPlay mediation and optional purchases through Google Play on Android or the Apple App Store on iOS and iPadOS.

Scope

This policy applies to Pack Sort: Box Puzzle on Android, iOS, and iPadOS, published by Nguyen Bui Ly under BuiLy Apps. It does not replace the privacy policies of Apple, Google, Unity, or advertising providers that process information through their services.

Privacy summary: The game does not require a player account or ask for your name, phone number, or email address during normal gameplay. Game progress and settings are saved on your device. The game contains banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads, and may offer optional in-app purchases through your platform's store. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, network information, approximate location, diagnostics, and interactions with ads, subject to applicable privacy choices and permissions.

Information processed

Game data stored on your device

The game stores your current level and puzzle, in-game coins, item inventory, lives and recovery times, daily reward counters, and sound or vibration preferences locally. It also stores reward identifiers and hashed purchase identifiers to help prevent duplicate grants. The game does not upload this saved progress to a player account or a BuiLy Apps game server.

Advertising and device data

Unity LevelPlay and participating advertising networks may collect or receive IP addresses, approximate location inferred from network information, device or advertising identifiers, device and app details, diagnostic information, and information about ad impressions, clicks, video views, and other interactions. Available data depends on your device, region, privacy choices, operating-system permissions, SDK configuration, and the network serving the ad. The game does not request precise GPS location.

Consent and tracking choices

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and present applicable privacy messages and privacy options. Consent and opt-out signals may be saved on your device and read by the advertising SDKs. Whether an ad can be requested is distinct from whether you have agreed to personalized advertising. On supported Apple devices, the game requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before requesting access to the advertising identifier for tracking. Denying tracking permission does not prevent normal gameplay; available advertising is subject to that decision and other privacy choices.

In-game currency and rewards

Coins, items, and lives are recorded as local game data. When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the advertising provider reports whether a reward was earned so the game can grant it and record applicable daily limits. In-game rewards do not require you to provide payment card details.

Optional in-app purchases

Where purchases are available in your version and store region, you may buy coin packs, packs containing coins and items, or Remove Ads through Google Play Billing on Android or Apple In-App Purchase on iOS and iPadOS, using Unity Purchasing. The platform store handles payment. The game verifies Android purchase receipts locally and uses StoreKit to verify Apple transactions on the device before granting content. It saves the granted content or Remove Ads entitlement together with a hashed purchase identifier to prevent duplicate grants. BuiLy Apps does not receive your full payment card number through the game or send your receipts to a BuiLy Apps purchase-verification server. Apple, Google, and Unity may process purchase and technical information under their own policies.

Support communications

If you email BuiLy Apps, we receive your email address and the message, screenshots, recordings, device details, or other information you choose to include.

How information is used

  • Run the game and preserve settings, puzzle progress, coins, items, and lives between sessions.
  • Grant earned rewards, apply daily reward limits, and prevent duplicate rewards.
  • Process optional purchases, grant purchased content, restore Remove Ads, and prevent duplicate purchase grants.
  • Select, deliver, limit, and measure advertisements.
  • Understand ad interactions, diagnose technical problems, and maintain advertising services.
  • Detect invalid traffic, fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

Advertising providers process data under their own policies and applicable legal requirements, including consent where required.

Advertising and third-party services

Pack Sort: Box Puzzle uses Unity LevelPlay for advertising mediation. Ads may be supplied by Unity, including Unity Ads and ironSource Ads, or another participating network. Google AdMob is configured as a mediated network for Android and iOS. Google's User Messaging Platform provides the applicable consent and privacy-option forms.

The providers describe their data practices and available choices here:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for Android
  • Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for iOS
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Optional purchases use the platform store through Unity Purchasing. See the Google Play Terms, Apple Media Services terms, and the providers' privacy policies for their processing of purchase-related information.

The participating networks may change as advertising configuration is updated. Only providers configured for the version you use can supply ads. A provider's data collection and retention are governed by its own privacy policy.

How information is shared

Advertising and technical information may be transmitted to Unity LevelPlay and the providers involved in delivering, measuring, or protecting an ad. These providers may use information for advertising, measurement, frequency capping, diagnostics, security, and fraud prevention as described in their policies and subject to applicable consent, opt-outs, and platform permissions.

Apple or Google, together with Unity Purchasing, process purchase information to complete purchases and manage eligible entitlements. Payment information is handled by the relevant store under its own terms.

BuiLy Apps does not sell the information you send directly in a support email. Support information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, or investigate misuse. Advertising and support providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to applicable safeguards.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or removed. On Android, you can clear the game's app storage or uninstall it. On iOS and iPadOS, deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app may preserve that data. Device or operating-system backups may retain or restore copies depending on your backup settings. Removing local game data can permanently remove your progress, coins, items, and lives.

Local purchase records remain with your game data. Google Play or the Apple App Store retains purchase history independently and can restore an eligible Remove Ads purchase for the same store account. Confirmed consumable purchases, including coins and item packs, are not re-granted through Restore Purchases. The game does not provide account-based cloud synchronization of consumable balances or game progress.

Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request, maintain relevant support or security records, or meet legal obligations. Advertising providers determine retention periods for data they process; consult their policies or use their privacy request channels.

Reasonable measures are used to protect information we handle. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's privacy

The game does not ask children to submit personal details. BuiLy Apps does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through gameplay. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate steps, including deletion where applicable.

Advertising services have their own age-related requirements and privacy protections. Parents and guardians can use device, Google account, and Apple family controls to manage app access, purchases, and available privacy settings.

Your privacy choices

  • On Android, use device and Google account privacy settings to manage your advertising identifier and available advertising-personalization controls.
  • On iOS and iPadOS, use the system's tracking settings to manage the game's tracking permission. ATT permission and consent to advertising personalization are separate choices.
  • When required by the consent service, use Ad Privacy in the game's Settings to reopen privacy options and change or withdraw applicable choices. Ads collected under previous choices are hidden for the remainder of that session; advertising is initialized with updated choices on a subsequent launch.
  • Choose whether to watch optional rewarded ads. Declining a rewarded ad means the associated reward is not granted.
  • Remove locally saved game data using the platform controls described above; review your device backup settings if you also want to remove backups.
  • Use Restore Purchases in the shop or Settings to restore an eligible Remove Ads purchase with the same platform store account. Removing ads disables banners and interstitials; optional rewarded videos remain available.
  • Email BuiLy Apps to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you submitted directly to us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, receive a copy of your information, or complain to a data protection authority. For information held by an advertising provider, use its privacy resources or contact us for help finding the appropriate channel. Limiting advertising data does not necessarily remove ads.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact Nguyen Bui Ly at nguyenbuily.it@gmail.com. Include “Pack Sort: Box Puzzle” in the subject. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or government identification.

This policy may be updated when game features, advertising providers, or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
