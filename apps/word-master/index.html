Official support channel

Get help

Email nguyenbuily.it@gmail.com. Include your device model, OS version, app version, and what happened.

Sign in issues

Check that the device has internet access, iCloud is enabled, and Sign in with Apple is available for the Apple ID. Close and reopen the app before trying again.

Purchases

If purchased content does not appear, wait briefly and reopen the app. If the charge completed but content is still missing, email support with the transaction details. Do not send payment card information.

Bug reports

Describe the last action before the problem, what you expected, and what appeared instead. A screenshot or short screen recording is especially helpful.
